Privacy Policy

Study Tracker · last updated 30 October 2026

Study Tracker collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

Study Tracker saves the following on your iPhone, and nowhere else:

  • the subjects you create, with their names, colours and icons
  • your tasks, with titles, deadlines, priorities and time estimates
  • your study sessions, with their length, date and productivity rating
  • whether you chose the light or dark appearance

This is written to your device's own storage. It is never transmitted anywhere, and the developer has no way to see it.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal information.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking or advertising software.
  • It contains no third-party code that collects data.
  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device deletes everything it stored. There is nothing held elsewhere to request or erase, because nothing was ever sent anywhere.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change described here.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or help with the app, can be sent to constiweber@icloud.com.

This page is the privacy policy and the support page for Study Tracker.
